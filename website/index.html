About Raunaq

Making top-quality fabrication products since 1975.

Our journey started in 1975 in Pune, Maharashtra. Since then we have built fabricated and machined products to international standards: base frames, base plates, machine bases, tanks, pressure vessels, fabricated column pipes and header pipes, along with machining of heavy components and fabricated parts for railway bogies.
